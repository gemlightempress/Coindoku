Answers to common questions about playing Coindoku, plus how to reach us.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Fill the board so every row, every column and every coloured box has each coin exactly once. Tap a square (it turns white), then tap a coin at the bottom to place it. A short tutorial shows you how the first time you play.

What are hearts?

Hearts are your mistakes. A wrong coin costs one heart and shows a red ring; you can take a wrong coin back with Erase. Lose your last heart and the puzzle ends, but you can watch a short video to keep going. Coin Master gives you only one heart.

How do boosters work?

Tap a booster first, then tap a square. Magical Hint fills that square, Golden Row / Golden Column / Box Reveal fill its row, column or box, Clock Freeze pauses a countdown for 30 seconds and Lucky Shield blocks your next wrong coin. Tap a booster again to cancel. Get more in the Shop, from Daily Rewards, Lucky Flip and achievements.

What is Daily Crown?

A new challenge every day, the same for every player. Each one mixes board size, difficulty, number of hearts, a booster limit and sometimes a timer. Daily Crown pays double coins plus a streak bonus for solving it on consecutive days.

How do I earn Panda Coins?

Solve puzzles (perfect solves earn more), claim Daily Rewards and achievement rewards, try Lucky Flip, or watch the optional video on the win screen to double your coins. You can also buy Panda Coin packs in the Shop.

I bought Panda Coins but didn’t receive them.

Purchases are delivered as soon as Google Play confirms them. If one is still pending or the game closed before it arrived, reopen the game: it checks for undelivered purchases automatically. If it still hasn’t arrived, contact us with your Player ID and the Google Play order number.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play Games on the welcome screen or tap Sync Now in Settings. Your save is stored in your Google account; sign in on your new phone and the game restores it. Guest progress is saved on the device only.

Where are the leaderboards?

Tap the leaderboard icon on any mode’s board screen, or open Settings → your profile card → Leaderboards. Leaderboards use Google Play Games, so you need to be signed in.

How do I turn reminders, music or vibration on or off?

Open Settings. Music, Sound, Vibration and Reminders each have their own switch. Reminders shows small in-game pop-ups when an achievement is almost complete or you set a new leaderboard best, and optional reminders on your device when your Daily Reward or achievement rewards are ready.

How do I change my player name?

Open Settings and tap the pen next to your name. Names can be up to 16 characters.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset Progress clears your progress on this device. This can’t be undone.

Why do I see ads?

Coindoku is free to play and is supported by advertising. You may see:

  • A banner advertisement
  • Advertisements between levels
  • Optional rewarded advertisements that you can choose to watch for bonuses, such as an extra flip or a revive

For more information about advertising and related data, please see our Privacy Policy.

The game only plays in portrait. Is that a bug?

No. Coindoku is designed specifically for vertical portrait play. The screen stays upright when you turn your device because the game does not support landscape orientation.

The game is running slowly or lagging. What should I do?

Close other apps, restart the game, and make sure your device has enough free memory.

How do I report a bug or problem?

Contact support with your device model, game version, and a description of the problem. Screenshots are helpful.
