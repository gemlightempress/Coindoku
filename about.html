Coindoku: Coin Sudoku Puzzle

Sudoku with coins: pick a coin, place it, and solve every row, column and box.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

About the Game

Welcome to Coindoku: Coin Sudoku Puzzle, sudoku played with beautiful coins. Fill every row, every column and every box so each coin appears exactly once. Tap a square, then tap a coin to place it. Every puzzle is generated fresh on your device and has exactly one solution, so it can always be solved by logic alone.

Four Ways to Play

  • Golden Classic: relaxed Easy puzzles on a 6×6 or 9×9 board.
  • Daily Crown: a new challenge every day, the same for every player, mixing board size, difficulty, hearts, booster limits and timers.
  • Prime Rush: race the countdown on Easy, Medium or Hard; time left over earns bonus coins.
  • Coin Master: Hard puzzles with one heart, one mistake ends your game; every correct coin grows your pot.

Rewards & Extras

Earn Panda Coins by solving puzzles, collect Daily Rewards and Lucky Flip prizes, unlock 100 achievements, collect ten coin themes, and use six helpful boosters in every mode, Hard puzzles included: Magical Hint, Lucky Shield, Clock Freeze, Box Reveal, Golden Row and Golden Column.
